# Merry

**Merry - your AI companion that listens, remembers, and gets things done. Fully local.**

Merry is a desktop application designed to run locally on your machine, keeping voice and note workflows private by default. It uses speech-to-text and language models to act as a personal AI assistant for dictation, notes, meetings and everyday writing.

## Features

- **Local-first and private:** Your voice and data stay on your device unless you explicitly configure an external service.
- **Fast dictation and speech-to-text:** Lightweight transcription routes turn speech into usable text quickly.
- **Cross-platform target:** Merry is planned for macOS, Windows and Linux releases.
- **Seamless desktop integration:** Hotkeys, microphone capture and paste workflows help Merry fit into the apps you already use.

## Getting Started

1. Install the Merry build for your operating system.
2. Open Merry and complete the permission/setup flow.
3. Configure local or operator-approved model endpoints when prompted.
4. Start speaking, capturing notes and recording meetings.
