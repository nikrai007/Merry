# Merry 🎙️✨

**Merry — your AI companion that listens, remembers, and gets things done. Fully local.**

Merry is a powerful desktop application built to run completely locally on your machine, ensuring complete privacy. It uses advanced speech-to-text and language models to act as your personal AI assistant.

## Features

- **Fully Local & Private:** Your voice and data never leave your device.
- **Fast Dictation & Speech-to-Text:** Powered by lightweight models (like Whisper and Parakeet) for fast, accurate transcription.
- **Cross-Platform:** Available for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
- **Seamless Integration:** Uses system-level key listeners, mic listeners, and fast paste to seamlessly interact with your OS.
